How to use it:

(1) Read the failure labels first. `never_reached_object` means the approach phase is
    wrong or too weak; the manipulation expressions are irrelevant until it is fixed.
    `pushed_away_from_goal` means the arm does reach the object and then drives it in the
    wrong direction, which is a geometry or sign error in the push term, not a gain
    problem. `object_not_moved` means contact is being made without force transfer.
    `stopped_short_of_goal` is the only label where increasing a gain is the obvious fix.

(2) Signed goal progress is the discriminating measure when every candidate scores zero
    success. Positive means the object ended closer to the goal than it started. A
    candidate at +0.05 and one at -0.05 have opposite faults, not different amounts of the
    same fault.

(3) Check the closest-approach distance against the contact threshold the policy declares.
    A policy whose gate opens at 0.06 m but whose arm never gets closer than 0.09 m has a
    phase that never fires, and its push expression has never been tested.

(4) Gate occupancy tells you whether a phase activated at all. A gate that crossed 0.5 in
    zero episodes is dead structure: either the threshold is wrong or the quantity it
    compares is not what you think it is.

(5) Parameters listed as not affecting the executed action are wasted capacity. Remove
    them, or connect them to the action. Note that the scale (`std`) parameters are always
    in this list: deterministic evaluation executes `tanh(mean)` and ignores `std`.

(6) Action saturation near zero on every axis means the controller is not limited by its
    output range, so increasing gains is unlikely to be the fix. Saturation near one means
    it is railed and the structure cannot ask for more.

(7) Observation fields are read by name through `self.obs_field(obs, "<name>")`. A name
    that does not exist raises immediately, so an unavailable quantity is an error rather
    than silently wrong numbers.

(8) Prefer fewer parameters. On this task, structures with at most ten parameters have
    scored roughly four times higher than those with twenty to thirty-six, despite fitting
    the expert no better. Extra sigmoid sharpness parameters in particular add plateaus to
    the loss surface without adding capability.
